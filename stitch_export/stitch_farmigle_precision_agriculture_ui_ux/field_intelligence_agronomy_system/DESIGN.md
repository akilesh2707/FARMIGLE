## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a balance of 70% geospatial field intelligence and 30% human agrarian stewardship. It serves agronomists, precision farm operators, and field managers who need clear data under direct sunlight and high-stress field conditions.

The aesthetic combines scientific precision with agrarian warmth. It avoids generic tech tropes such as glowing neon accents, heavy dropshadows, or frosted glassmorphism. Instead, it relies on paper-like textural calm, disciplined telemetry, tactile boundaries, and high-legibility typographic hierarchies. The interface balances high-density data visualizations (NDVI, soil moisture, canopy nitrogen, multispectral plots) with clear operational workflows, maintaining cultural and linguistic legibility across both English and Tamil scripts.

## Colors
The palette draws from botanical and pedological environments, optimized for outdoor readability and low ocular strain during continuous operation.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary Brand (`#183C32`)**: Dense forest green used for structural branding, primary action triggers, critical state selections, and dominant navigational anchors.
- **Deep Pine (`#244F43`)**: Supporting shade for active interactive states, table headers, and structural division lines.
- **Sage (`#91A995`)**: Botanical mid-tone for auxiliary graphs, secondary highlights, active toggles, and data plots.
- **Accent Clay (`#B66B4C`)**: Earth-derived accent used for urgent telemetry points, human intervention markers, manual input notifications, and selected geospatial pins.
- **Sand (`#DCCEB5`)**: Ground tone for soft data zones, perimeter highlights, and telemetry band indicators.

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Background Warm Paper (`#F7F5EF`)**: Base canvas tone replicating archival agricultural records and unbleached field journals. Avoids high-glare pure white across global views.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Strictly reserved for elevated interactive modules, active telemetry cards, modals, and spatial control overlays.
- **Mist (`#E7ECE5`)**: Intermediate structural canvas for panel dividers, inactive card zones, nested data bays, and control track backgrounds.
- **Border Structural (`#E3E5DF`)**: Calibrated boundary color for hairline dividers and card perimeters, providing structure without heavy contrast.
- **Text Ink (`#202925`)**: Primary deep-charcoal green-black for headers and high-priority telemetry readouts.
- **Text Secondary (`#68736D`)**: Balanced neutral for explanatory field copy, secondary metric units, and metadata.
- **Text Muted (`#8A928D`)**: De-emphasized neutral for timestamps, disabled elements, structural guides, and secondary axis markers.

### Agricultural Operational States
- **Harvest Ready (`#3F7658`)**: Optimal biological maturity, normal moisture, completed cycles.
- **Near Ready (`#B68A3A`)**: Transitioning phase, advisory alert, moderate hydrologic stress.
- **Health Concern (`#A95545`)**: Critical pest ingress, canopy blight, sensor disconnect, threshold breach.
- **Information (`#52707A`)**: Geospatial updates, satellite pass schedule, ambient weather telemetry.

## Typography
The typographic system relies entirely on IBM Plex Sans across all functional tiers, chosen for its engineering balance, open counters, clear terminal cuts, and legible rendering in dense tabular formats.

### Script Inclusivity & Alignment
- **Bilingual English & Tamil Support**: IBM Plex Sans maintains glyph proportions and structural baseline balance with Tamil script extensions. In Tamil contexts, avoid artificial uppercase transforms (`text-transform: uppercase`) applied to `technical-label`; use `font-weight: 600` at `12px` to ensure the complex conjuncts and loop terminals render cleanly without clipping.
- **Tabular Figures**: All data displays, geospatial coordinates (Lat/Long), NDVI metrics, and moisture indices must use `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to prevent layout jitter during live stream ingestion.
- **Letter Spacing Discipline**: Technical tags, telemetry indicators, and status badges utilize strict `0.08em` tracking in uppercase English. Body text and narrative agronomy advice maintain zero tracking for continuous reading speed.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-column structural grid system built on an 8px base rhythm (with a 4px secondary sub-grid for dense spatial telemetry displays).

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 40px (`2.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Split-view configurations anchor geospatial map telemetry on a fixed or proportional 70% viewport pane, dedicating 30% to field operational data and diagnostic controls.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) margins. The map-to-telemetry split transitions to an interactive collapsible bottom sheet or a balanced 50/50 dual pane.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) margins. Spatial overlays take priority, transforming detailed parameter lists into stacked horizontal scroll trays or swipable cards.

### Spacing Usage
- `space-xs` (4px): Metric label-to-value micro offsets, status indicator dot margins, inline unit padding.
- `space-sm` (8px): Button internal vertical padding, icon-to-label separation, chip inner horizontal clearance.
- `space-md` (16px): Structural card interior padding, field layout input gaps, list-item separations.
- `space-lg` (24px): Inter-card margins, telemetry grid gaps, section header separations.
- `space-xl` (36px): Primary content zone divisions, spatial inspector drawer margins.
- `space-2xl` (56px): Page header boundaries and major architectural transitions.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through tonal layering and structural borders, avoiding deep drop shadows, blurry skeuomorphism, and translucent frosted glass. 

### Surface Tiers
1. **Tier 0 (Base Earth)**: Canvas tone `#F7F5EF` representing unselected background fields.
2. **Tier 1 (Structural Inset)**: Tint `#E7ECE5` applied to static sidebars, tool drawers, map background voids, and inactive telemetry wells.
3. **Tier 2 (Active Foreground)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` for active cards, input boxes, detail view modules, and data panels. Delineated by a mandatory 1px hairline border in `#E3E5DF`.
4. **Tier 3 (Floating Tactical Overlays)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` elevated over GIS maps and vector plots for map controls, polygon drawing HUDs, and filter trays. Uses an ambient, low-profile soil shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(32, 41, 37, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(32, 41, 37, 0.04)`.
5. **Tier 4 (Critical Dialogs / Intervention Modals)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px border in `#DCCEB5`, paired with an ambient tinted drop shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(24, 60, 50, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
Shapes use restrained, purposeful geometry. Corners remain grounded, avoiding overly round or pill-shaped consumer UI styles.

### Dimensional Radii Rules
- **Tactile Triggers (Buttons & Selectors)**: Exactly `7px` radius for physical utility and grip.
- **Form Controls (Inputs, Dropdowns, Datepickers)**: Exactly `8px` radius.
- **Card Containers & Modules**: Exactly `10px` radius.
- **App Panels, Main Canvas Surfaces, & Floating Drawers**: Exactly `12px` radius.
- **Map Viewports & Full Sensor Polygons**: Exactly `12px` radius when framed inside desktop modules; `0px` when docked directly to viewport margins.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Only status indicator pips and count tags smaller than 20px in height use a fully rounded `9999px` boundary.

## Components

- **Primary**: Background `#183C32`, Text `#FFFFFF`, Border 1px solid `#183C32`, Radius `7px`, Height `40px` (Desktop) / `44px` (Mobile touch targets). Active state drops to `#244F43`.
- **Secondary**: Background `#FFFFFF`, Text `#183C32`, Border 1px solid `#E3E5DF`, Radius `7px`. Hover transitions surface to `#E7ECE5`.
- **Destructive / Urgent**: Background `#FFFFFF`, Text `#A95545`, Border 1px solid `#A95545`. Active fills with `#A95545` and Text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Field Action (Tertiary Accent)**: Background `#B66B4C`, Text `#FFFFFF`, used strictly for dispatch operations, harvest confirmations, and manual drone launch commands.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Dimension**: Height `28px`, padding `0 10px`, radius `7px`.
- **Telemetry Filter**: Background `#E7ECE5`, Text `#202925`, Border 1px solid `#E3E5DF`.
- **Selected State**: Background `#183C32`, Text `#FFFFFF`, Border 1px solid `#183C32`.
- **Indicator Chips**: Include a 6px circular dot on the leading edge displaying operational state tokens (e.g., `#3F7658` for Harvest Ready).

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- **Construction**: Background `#FFFFFF`, Border 1px solid `#E3E5DF`, Radius `10px`, Padding `16px` or `20px`.
- **Section Headers**: Typography `section-title` in `#202925`, anchored by an optional right-aligned `technical-label` in `#8A928D`.
- **Data Callout Split**: Border-top or border-left 1px solid `#E7ECE5` isolating metric figures (`metric-value`) from narrative diagnostic recommendations.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height `42px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E3E5DF`, radius `8px`, typography `body-default` in `#202925`.
- **Focus State**: Border 1.5px solid `#183C32`, zero outline glow, subtle background tint `#F7F5EF`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px structural footprint, 1.5px border `#68736D`, checked fill `#183C32` with `#FFFFFF` mark, radius `4px` for checkboxes and `50%` for radios.

### Specialized Precision Agriculture Components
- **Geospatial Map HUD**: Floating tool panel mounted directly over the map canvas. Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E3E5DF`, radius `12px`, padding `8px`, housing layer toggles (NDVI, RGB, Thermal, Moisture).
- **Zonal Health Ribbon**: Linear status tracker displaying field vegetative indexes using categorical bands: `#A95545` (Critical), `#B68A3A` (Moderate), and `#3F7658` (Vigorous), set at 6px height with 3px radii.
- **Telemetry Gauge Tile**: Minimal numeric tile for tractor CAN bus and loT sensor readouts: small uppercase technical label on top, 28px tabular figure in middle, and an inline status pip on the bottom edge.
- **Language Switch Toggle**: Accessible dual control anchoring English (`EN`) and Tamil (`தமிழ்`), maintaining 32px height, 1px border `#E3E5DF`, and `#183C32` active background indicator.
